# NJOY article research and editorial notes

Prepared on 12 October 2026 for 12 new editorial guides. Content is original; recipe combinations are serving suggestions, not laboratory-tested recipes or nutrition prescriptions. The current delivered product label is the reference for ingredients, allergen statements, nutrition and storage. No competitor superiority rankings, unverifiable certifications, disease claims or fitness outcomes are included.

## Verified primary sources

- [MYFITNESS official website](https://myfitness.in/): the official range includes smooth and crunchy Original peanut butter and chocolate variants. Variant-specific information changes; the articles do not reproduce volatile prices, percentages or promotional superiority claims.
- [MYFITNESS Original Crunchy product page](https://myfitness.in/products/myfitness-original-peanut-butter-crunchy): a specific official product page is available for checking the current variant. Research showed inconsistent protein headlines across page elements; therefore no MYFITNESS protein figure is asserted in the content.
- [Pintola official website](https://pintola.in/): current direct product-page access redirected to an "Opening Soon / Website Under Maintenance" page. Cached search results mentioned a natural organic peanut butter product, but no current formula, price or certification claim is asserted. The buying article links to the official brand as a further-reading reference, not proof of a current specification.
- [FSSAI labelling and display FAQs](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/FAQ_Labelling_Display_01_11_2023.pdf): explains single-ingredient foods and alternatives for communicating allergens that may arise from cross-contact. Articles use it as an Indian label-reading reference, not a statement that every pictured product has a specific certification.
- [USDA MyPlate Protein Foods](https://www.myplate.gov/web/web/eat-healthy/protein-foods): nuts and nut butters appear in the Protein Foods group; the source encourages varied choices. No daily target or USDA ounce-equivalent is represented as grams of protein. The 24g-per-100g calculation in the article is explicitly fictional and mathematically checked (15g portion = 3.6g, 20g portion = 4.8g).
- [Harvard Nutrition Source: Almonds](https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/food-features/almonds/): identifies vitamin E, fibre and monounsaturated fats among almond nutrients; describes almonds as energy-dense. These general whole-almond observations are not treated as NJOY laboratory values or a claim that almond butter is always better than peanut butter. The source also gives almond butter serving ideas such as toast, fruit and oats.
- [Smucker's official FAQs](https://www.smuckers.com/frequently-asked-questions): discusses peanut butter oil separation and says refrigeration slows it for its own products. The article explicitly avoids transferring that brand's refrigeration rules or shelf life to NJOY.
- [FDA major food allergens](https://www.fda.gov/industry/fda-basics-industry/what-major-food-allergen): identifies peanuts and tree nuts among major allergens. Used for general allergen awareness, not to describe US law as Indian labelling law.
- [FDA food allergies](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/food-allergies-what-you-need-know): explains the importance of checking ingredients and avoiding known allergens. Content never assumes a tree nut or seed product is safe for a peanut-allergic recipient.
- [FDA food-storage guidance](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/are-you-storing-food-safely): recommends checking package storage directions and explains that safety cannot always be determined from appearance or smell. Articles do not use US date terminology to override the Indian package label.
- [FDA kitchen food safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/food-safety-your-kitchen): provides general shopping, storage and meal-preparation guidance. Prepared recipes are treated separately from the stored original product.
- [EPA recycling basics](https://www.epa.gov/recycle/recycling-basics-and-benefits): source reduction and reuse sit before recycling in its hierarchy. No emissions savings or environmental score is assigned to NJOY.
- [EPA common recyclables: glass](https://www.epa.gov/recycle/how-do-i-recycle-common-recyclables): explains that glass guidance is general and local collection programmes have their own rules. US collection instructions are not applied as Indian municipal rules.
- [EPA waste actions](https://www.epa.gov/climate-change/what-you-can-do-about-climate-change-waste): presents refuse, reduce, reuse, repair and recycle as practical actions. Packaging is not described as zero-waste or sustainable without qualification.
- [NJOY Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/nut_delicacy/): URL already used by the website and supplied in the task. Linked for brand/serving inspiration, not as evidence of nutrition or product safety claims. No claims about individual recent posts are made.

## Brand-specific information

The owner supplied a **2-month butter shelf life**. Content states this only where relevant to storage. It does not claim that opening or refrigeration restarts the period. Brand product IDs and article images were matched against `products.js` and existing `assets/catalog/*-800.webp` files; no new image download or bandwidth cost is introduced.

## Coverage and duplication

Existing blog filenames cover generic labels, breakfast ideas, palm oil, almond benefits, weight loss, stone grinding, pistachio introduction and pistachio versus peanut butter. The new guides take distinct search intents: Indian online buying comparisons; smooth versus crunchy uses; stirring a separated jar; building ingredient preferences; toast assembly; almond versus peanut kitchen value; pistachio recipe uses; label-based portion calculations; a storage routine; corporate Diwali order logistics; chocolate pairing; and practical jar reuse.

Sources must be rendered as clearly labelled clickable further-reading links. They are not an affiliation or endorsement. Individual pages should also render FAQs in visible text, a clear author/publisher byline, actual publication date, related products and navigation back to the journal. Article schema should match the rendered title and text. Do not promise rich results or Google ranking improvements.
