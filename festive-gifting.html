Frequently Asked Questions

Artisanal Care & Direct Delivery

Why is there natural oil separation on top of the jar?

Oil separation is the true signature of 100% pure, unadulterated nut butter. Because we never add hydrogenated palm oil or chemical emulsifiers, the natural healthy oils rise to the top. Simply give it a gentle stir before spreading.

How does bulk ordering work?

You can select Bulk Order on any product. Click 'Request Bulk Quote on WhatsApp' and our concierge team (+91 95125 12151) will immediately share customized wholesale pricing and logistics timelines based on your order quantity.

What is the shelf life and storage recommendation?

Our stone-ground nut butters stay fresh for 6 months at cool room temperature. For a firmer, thicker texture, you may refrigerate your jar after giving it an initial stir.
