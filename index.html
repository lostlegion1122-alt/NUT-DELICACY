Knowledge & Transparency

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Nut Delicacy different from regular supermarket nut butters?

Most commercial nut butters use high-speed steel blades that heat nuts above 60°C and add hydrogenated palm oil, preservatives, and cane sugar. Nut Delicacy uses traditional granite stone mills operating below 28°C with exactly 100% whole organic nuts and zero shortcuts.

Why do you have a zero palm oil guarantee?

Palm oil is added to commercial spreads only to chemically prevent natural oil separation. We refuse to compromise your health. 100% of the healthy fats in our jars come directly from whole roasted nuts.

Why is there oil separation on top of my jar?

Natural oil separation is proof of pure, unadulterated nut butter. Because there are no hydrogenated stabilizers, the natural nut oil rises over time. Simply give it a gentle stir before enjoying.
