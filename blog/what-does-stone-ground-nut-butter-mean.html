What Does Stone-Ground Nut Butter Mean? The Granite Milling Ritual

By Nut Delicacy Master Roaster • August 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Walk down any commercial supermarket aisle and you will find nut butters mass-produced by high-speed steel blenders in mere seconds. Yet, at Nut Delicacy, our nut butters take hours to mill slowly between heavy rotating granite stones. Why do we insist on this traditional method?

1. The Problem with Industrial High-Speed Milling

Industrial commercial blenders rely on sharp stainless steel blades spinning at thousands of RPMs. While fast and cheap, this process generates intense localized friction heat (often exceeding 60°C to 70°C). This heat degrades sensitive Vitamin E, oxidizes delicate Omega-3 and Omega-6 fatty acids, and burns the delicate roasted nut aroma into a flat, scorched paste.

2. The Granite Stone-Ground Process

Stone-grinding is an ancient, gentle art. Slow-moving cylindrical granite millstones roll continuously over a granite base bed. The whole roasted nuts are steadily sheared, crushed, and smoothed under immense natural stone pressure.

  • Low Temperature (<28°C): Cold milling ensures that essential fatty acids, polyphenols, and enzymes remain completely undamaged.
  • Natural Oil Emulsion: The nuts naturally release their own luxurious oils without requiring added palm oil, hydrogenated fats, or emulsifiers.
  • Velvety Micro-Texture: Yields a silky, luxurious mouthfeel with zero chalkiness or artificial gumminess.

3. 100% Purity Guarantee

Because stone-milling creates its own natural emulsion, there is never any need for shortcuts. Our jars contain zero palm oil, zero preservatives, and zero added sugars.
